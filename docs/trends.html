What this shows

Price change for comparable insurance policies. The ABS prices home and motor vehicle insurance each month using web-scraped price quotes for a range of customer profiles, so the index shows how prices change. The category covers comprehensive insurance for dwellings, home contents and motor vehicles, and compulsory third party insurance.

What it does not show

What households pay in total, which also moves with sums insured, coverage choices and risk. Individual premiums can change by more or less than the index. It does not show separate figures for home and car insurance, because the ABS does not publish them below this level. It also does not show why prices changed.

Why prices rose so fast in 2023–24

Annual growth in insurance prices peaked at 16.4% in the year to the March quarter 2024. Published sources point to three main causes. Reinsurance costs rose sharply. The Actuaries Institute found that home insurance premium increases in the year to March 2024 were primarily a consequence of higher reinsurance costs during 20231, and the Reserve Bank reported that general insurers’ reinsurance expenses rose by over 50% between June 2018 and June 20232. Disaster losses were large, with the 2022 floods causing $7.17 billion in insured losses3. Building costs also rose quickly. The Insurance Council reports that annual construction cost inflation peaked above 11% in 20224. Price growth slowed to below 3% in the second half of 2025, and by March 2026 the Reserve Bank described a softening reinsurance market5.

Insurance compared with other household costs

Price change for insurance and for each of the 11 groups that make up the Consumer Price Index, over the same period.

md`Price change from the ${periodName(categoryData[0].from)} to the ${periodName(categoryData[0].to)}. The dashed line marks the all-groups CPI.`
categoryChart(Plot, categoryData, {width})

What this shows

How insurance price growth compares with each part of household spending measured by the CPI. Insurance is a single CPI expenditure class. The other bars are the 11 CPI groups, each an average of many items, so some items within a group rise faster or slower than the group as a whole. The insurance and financial services group combines insurance with deposit and loan fees and other financial services. Those fees rose more slowly, so the group rose less than insurance alone.

What it does not show

Why prices changed, or the items within each group. Building and vehicle repair costs, which feed into insurance claims, are covered in Why premiums change.

Latest figures

The figures behind the charts, for quoting and checking.

latestTable = {
  const five = new Map(changeOver(quarterly, 5).map(d => [d.series, d.v]));
  const ten = new Map(changeOver(quarterly, 10).map(d => [d.series, d.v]));
  const order = Object.keys(labels);
  const latest = order.map(s => {
    const rows = quarterly.filter(d => d.series === s);
    return rows.length ? rows.reduce((a, b) => (a.date > b.date ? a : b)) : null;
  }).filter(Boolean);
  return html`<div class="table-scroll"><table class="data-table">
    <thead><tr><th scope="col">Series</th><th scope="col">Latest quarter</th><th scope="col">Annual change (quarter)</th><th scope="col">5-year change</th><th scope="col">10-year change</th></tr></thead>
    <tbody>${latest.map(d => html`<tr class=${d.series === "Insurance" ? "hl" : ""}>
      <th scope="row">${labels[d.series]}</th><td>${periodName(d.period)}</td>
      <td>${fmt(d.annual_change)}</td><td>${fmt(five.get(d.series))}</td><td>${fmt(ten.get(d.series))}</td></tr>`)}
    </tbody></table></div>`;
}

Annual change compares the latest quarter with the same quarter a year earlier. It can differ from the monthly headline figure (5.4% for insurance in the year to September 2026), which compares single months.

About these figures

What the insurance index measures

The CPI insurance expenditure class covers comprehensive insurance for dwellings, home contents and motor vehicles, and compulsory third party motor vehicle insurance. Health insurance is not included. The ABS classifies it in the Health group, under Medical and hospital services.6, paras. 6.33, 8.126

Insurance, or insurance and financial services?

“Insurance” is an expenditure class, the lowest level at which the ABS publishes price indexes. “Insurance and financial services” is a broader CPI group that adds deposit and loan fees and other financial services. The two measure different things.

The 2000 spike

The insurance index rose by about 30% in the year to June 2000. The ABS attributed this mainly to the GST. Under the method used then, the CPI valued insurance as the net service households receive, roughly premiums less claims. Taxes on premiums are a larger share of that smaller base, so the GST moved the index by more than it moved the premiums households paid.7

Monthly and quarterly figures

Since November 2025 the ABS has published a complete monthly CPI. The quarterly CPI is the average of the three months in each quarter. This page uses ABS-published monthly annual changes for the headline figure and quarterly index numbers for long-run history.
