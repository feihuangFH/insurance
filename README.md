# Insurance Explained

[![License: CC BY 4.0](https://img.shields.io/badge/License-CC%20BY%204.0-lightgrey.svg)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)

Understanding insurance prices, risks and affordability in Australia. The site tracks insurance price inflation with official ABS data, explains what drives premiums, and brings together the evidence published by regulators and industry bodies.

**Live site: [insurance.feihuang.org](https://insurance.feihuang.org)**

Developed by [Fei Huang](https://www.feihuang.org), UNSW Sydney.

## Contents

| Page | What it covers |
|---|---|
| [Trends](https://insurance.feihuang.org/trends.html) | Insurance prices compared with overall inflation and other household costs, from the ABS Consumer Price Index |
| [Why premiums change](https://insurance.feihuang.org/why-premiums-change.html) | The actuarial pricing framework and the drivers of premiums |
| [Explainers](https://insurance.feihuang.org/explainers.html) | Short, sourced answers on premiums, inflation, natural disasters, underinsurance and what consumers can do |
| [Key figures](https://insurance.feihuang.org/key-figures.html) | The latest figures and plain definitions |
| [Commentary](https://insurance.feihuang.org/commentary.html) | Articles and media |

## Data

ABS figures are retrieved from the [ABS Data API](https://www.abs.gov.au/about/data-services/application-programming-interfaces-apis/data-api-user-guide) (dataflows CPI and CPI_WEIGHTS) and are available for download on the Trends page. Figures from other organisations are cited and linked to their original sources.
